The Root Cause & Operational Reality

Operational Blindness Creates the "Forgotten Patient"

Patients don't get forgotten because clinical staff don't care — they get forgotten because hospital IT systems trap information within departmental silos.

PAIN POINT 01
Department Silos & Blind Handoffs
Zero cross-visibility between triage, inpatient wards, and diagnostic labs. When a blood test or MRI stalls, ward coordinators remain completely unaware until patients complain or deteriorate.
PAIN POINT 02
Compounding Non-Linear Latencies
A minor 25-minute delay in a stat laboratory test spirals into a 4+ hour emergency department bed block and ambulance bypass condition downstream.
PAIN POINT 03
Reactive, Retrospective Reporting
Traditional EHR reporting and retrospective dashboards analyze dwell times hours or days after patients suffered, rather than triggering interventions while they are still waiting in the hall.
